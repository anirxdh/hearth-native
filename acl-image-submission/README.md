# RUA — Screenshot Submission (HHS/ACL Caregiver AI Prize Challenge)

RUA is a calm, privacy-first productivity app for family caregivers: one place that keeps every person you care for — their medications and refill runways, vitals and trends, appointments, records, and emergency information — organized and ready to share on paper or PDF. AI does the tedious parts (reading pill-bottle labels on-device, drafting insurance appeals and other paperwork, prepping visit sheets, answering questions grounded only in the family's own records) while a human confirms every fact before anything is saved or sent. Everything runs on demo data for the demo family — Maria caring for her dad Robert, mom Eleanor, and aunt Rosa — and every capture shows the RUA brand.

Two folders tell the same story on two surfaces: `web/` (desktop app, 57 PNGs at 2940x1912) and `mobile/` (native iOS app, 47 PNGs at 1206x2622, iPhone 17 Pro). Files with a `-highlight` suffix are annotated copies (red box calling out the feature); `-2` suffixes are the lower half of the same screen.
